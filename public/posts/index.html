The entire course placement layer exists solely because of the domain: academic scheduling has prerequisites (a directed dependency graph), term offerings (courses only exist in Fall or Spring), department enrollment caps (no more than 2 CSC courses/semester in years 1–2), and standing requirements. None of these constraints map onto generic CRUD patterns. The architecture adds a dedicated constraint validation layer (canPlaceCourse, lines 233–308) that sits between any user or algorithm action and the state mutation.
